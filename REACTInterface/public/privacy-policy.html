Privacy Policy for REACT Cloud

Last updated: November 20, 2026

REACT Cloud ("the Application") is a scientific research platform for chemical reaction mechanism generation, molecular structure management, and thermodynamic analysis.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Firebase Authentication (including Facebook, Google, GitHub, or Email/Password), we collect only the basic account identifiers provided by your chosen identity provider:

  • Display name
  • Email address
  • Unique user identifier (UID) and optional profile picture URL

We also store the scientific data you explicitly create or upload in your personal workspace (molecules, substructures, reaction patterns, mechanism pathways, and execution logs).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your session and isolate your personal chemical database workspace.
  • To execute reaction mechanism generation jobs and store your output artifacts.
  • To maintain service security, prevent abuse, and enforce resource quotas.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Sharing

Your account metadata and workspace data are stored securely on Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Firestore and Google Cloud Storage). We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and workspace data for as long as your account remains active. You may request complete deletion of your account and associated data at any time by following the instructions on our User Data Deletion page.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the system administrator at edward.blurock@gmail.com.
